About Packfast
Packfast is an asset-based carrier with 35+ years of combined experience. We move freight between Québec / Ontario and across the U.S. with reliable, on-time service.
We offer in-house customs clearance at competitive prices — so your freight is border-ready right away. No delays, no third-party coordination. Our team handles the paperwork so your load crosses without interruption.
